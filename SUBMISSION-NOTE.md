# Internship submission note

I built a responsive, single-page website for Endloops to explain its financial automation offering to accountants, auditors, finance teams and property management companies. The page separates the company's end-to-end financial solutions from its standalone tools, then explains the document-processing workflow and the role of professional review.

My most important design decision was to keep the visual language restrained and clear. A neutral background, dark navy typography and limited teal/green accents help the site feel appropriate for a business handling detailed financial work. I also used a dashboard-style illustration to make the idea of document preparation and review easier to understand, while keeping its sample figures clearly illustrative.

With more time, I would validate the content and visual direction with Endloops, connect the enquiry form to an approved backend, and conduct broader accessibility and browser testing. The project uses React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion and Lucide React. AI assistance was used for development support and copy refinement; the implementation and design decisions should be reviewed and understood before submission.
